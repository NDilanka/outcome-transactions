# Exp 6 blind run: results

Generated by `research/exp6-executed/blind_results.py` from the private run files. Aggregates only.

## Scope

- 238 blind end states from 34 tasks (generator labels: 170 wrong, 68 correct).
- Specs run as written (`specexec.py`). OSWorld checker run where its functions read a file.
- End states are the normalized copies: 15 workbooks were re-saved and recalculated by LibreOffice because the generator dropped cached formula values (`normalize_states.py`, checks in `normalize_report.json`). The unnormalized run is shown below for comparison.
- The sample is below the 200-wrong-state gate (see Gates).

## Headline

- Wrong accepts, labels as claimed: 3/170 (1.8%); 95% upper bound 5.07%.
- Wrong accepts, after label corrections: 2/173 (1.2%); 95% upper bound 4.11%.
- Wrong accepts, if the a9f325aa formula state counts as wrong: 3/174 (1.7%); upper bound 4.96%.
- Wrong accepts in the three LibreOffice families only (the executor has readers there; other families are UNDECIDED or NOT_RUNNABLE by construction): 2/86 (2.3%); upper bound 8.15%.
- Correct states rejected by the executor, after corrections: 6/65 (9.2%). Of these, 2 come from an executor bug, 1 from a spec contradiction, 2 from a renderer question, and 1 from an ambiguous reading.
- Correct states not accepted (REJECT or UNDECIDED), after corrections: 50/65 (76.9%).
